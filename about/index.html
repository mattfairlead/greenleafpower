About Us

Our Mission

To develop and operate high-performing renewable energy facilities that deliver clean, reliable power while creating sustainable value for our communities, employees, and stakeholders.

Our Vision

To be the leading operator of biomass and renewable energy facilities in North America, setting the standard for operational excellence, environmental stewardship, and community partnership.

Our Values

Safety, integrity, environmental responsibility, and operational excellence guide every decision we make — from how we run our plants to how we support the communities we serve.

Three Decades of Renewable Energy Leadership

Greenleaf Power has been at the forefront of biomass energy since the early 1990s. We develop, acquire, and operate renewable energy facilities across the United States, converting waste wood materials into clean, reliable electricity.

Our team of experienced power plant operators, engineers, and environmental specialists brings decades of expertise to every facility. We are committed to safe operations, environmental compliance, and generating value for the communities where we operate.

With a portfolio of facilities spanning multiple states, Greenleaf Power is a proven partner for utilities seeking dependable, renewable baseload power — and a valued employer in the communities we call home.
