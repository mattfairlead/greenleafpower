About Us

consistent energy generation

Greenleaf Power meets North America’s growing energy needs through the investment in, and development of reliable, green energy sources. Our goal is to provide carbon-neutral energy production that is consistent and ''count-on-able'' unlike other types of renewable energy.

Leadership

A leader in the production of green energy from biomass

Renewable resources

Cogeneration plants generate green energy from renewable sources, for better air quality and a healthier environment.

our mission

Greenleaf Power is a leading North American energy transition company focused on recycling waste into renewable, reliable energy.

Having successfully scaled its North American renewable energy footprint, Greenleaf plans to further develop clean, responsible ecosystems at each site with biochar, green hydrogen, EV charging, and more.
